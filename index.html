Delivery and pickup

All orders are fulfilled in person at school. Each product page lists an estimated delivery date, which is updated as print batches are scheduled. On checkout you will be asked for your name, homeroom, and grade so your order can be routed to you. Payment is cash only, due when you receive your item.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Cash only, in person. There is no online payment — you pay when your item is delivered or picked up.

When do I get my item?

Each product page shows an estimated delivery date. Pre-order items are printed in batches and delivered on or around the listed date. You can track your order status under My orders.

Who can order?

NextPrints currently serves students in grades 8–12 only. You must register with a verified school email address (@intermetro.edu) to place an order.

How do pre-orders work?

Pre-order pricing is available until the listed end date. After that, the product switches to its regular price. Pre-order items are delivered in batches starting on the estimated delivery date.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes. You can cancel a pending order from the My orders page before it is confirmed.
